🎉 Trivia Night

Spin the wheel, buzz in, and battle it out across six categories.

🌍 Geography 🏛️ History 🔬 Science ⚽ Sports ➗ Math 💡 General 🃏 Joker
🖥️

TV Display

Open on the laptop connected to the big screen. Shows the wheel, questions, and buzz-in order.
🎙️

Host Controller

Open on your own phone to run the game — pick teams, reveal answers, and keep score.
📱

Player View

Players scan the QR code on the TV once teams are assigned, then buzz in from here.

Mode is set in js/config.js (LAN or online) — see README.md for setup.
